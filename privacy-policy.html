Privacy Policy

Updated October 2026 On-Device Only

What wip Collects

wip does not collect your notes or personal information. There is no account and no sign-in, and your content never leaves your iPad through wip. The only data that can ever be sent is an anonymous crash report, and only if you turn that on (see Crash Reports).

Your Notes Stay on Your iPad

Notebooks, folders, handwriting, text, stickers, arrows, imported PDFs, imported fonts and photos you place on pages are stored locally inside the app on your iPad. wip has no servers and does not sync or upload this content.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPad to a computer, iPadOS may include wip's data in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under your device settings and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Photos and Files

When you add a photo, wip uses the system photo picker (PhotosPicker). You choose exactly which images to share, and wip cannot see the rest of your photo library. Selected photos are copied into the app's private storage.

PDFs and fonts you import through the Files picker are copied into the app's private storage so they stay available offline. wip only accesses the files you select.

Crash Reports (Optional)

You can choose to share anonymous crash reports in wip's Settings. This is off by default. When it is on, a report is sent to our crash reporting provider, Sentry, if the app crashes or hits a serious error.

A crash report contains technical information only: the app version, iPadOS version, device model, the code location of the error and a short technical log, plus a random identifier for this installation so repeated problems can be counted. That identifier is not linked to you. IP addresses and locations are not stored. Reports do not contain your notes, drawings, PDFs, photos, notebook titles, your name or any account identifier. We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

If you take part in beta testing through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash logs and any feedback you choose to submit with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

No Tracking or Advertising

wip contains no advertising and no analytics. It does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your content stays on your iPad until you delete it. Deleting a notebook or page removes it from the app. Uninstalling wip permanently deletes all of its data from your iPad (copies in your own device backups are managed by you).

Crash reports, if enabled, are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service wip uses is Sentry for optional crash reporting, described above. Sentry processes crash reports on our behalf and only to provide crash diagnostics.

Children

wip is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
