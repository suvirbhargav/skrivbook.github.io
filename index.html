About Skrivbook

Skrivbook is an iPad app for handwritten notes and PDF annotation. Create notebooks with the paper you like, import PDFs such as lecture slides, planners or forms, and write on them with Apple Pencil, just like on paper.

There is no account, no cloud service and no tracking. Your notes are stored on your iPad and nowhere else.

Features

  • Natural handwriting with Apple Pencil: pens, highlighter, eraser and lasso, with undo and redo
  • Import PDFs and write directly on the pages, including working links in interactive planners
  • Notebooks and folders with cover colours, favourites and multiple paper templates
  • Text boxes with your own imported fonts
  • Stickers, photos, shapes and smart arrows to sketch diagrams
  • Pinch to zoom, page navigation and search across your documents

Private by Design

Skrivbook works entirely offline. Notebooks, drawings, imported PDFs, fonts and photos are saved inside the app on your iPad. Skrivbook has no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it never sends your content anywhere.

When you add a photo, iPadOS lets you pick exactly which images to share, so Skrivbook never sees the rest of your photo library.

If you choose to, you can share anonymous crash reports from Settings to help fix bugs. This is off by default and never includes your notes.

Beta Testing

Skrivbook is currently in beta through Apple's TestFlight. It requires an iPad running iPadOS 17 or later; Apple Pencil is recommended but not required.

As a beta tester you can send feedback and screenshots directly from the TestFlight app. Because this is pre-release software, please keep copies of anything important elsewhere.
